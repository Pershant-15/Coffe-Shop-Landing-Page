Know Your Coffee

Six cups, explained

Every coffee starts with the same beans and ends up very different in the cup. The difference is how much milk, water or ice goes in with the espresso. Here is what each of our classics is, and how it is made.

01

Espresso

Espresso is coffee in its most concentrated form. Hot water is forced under pressure through finely ground beans, producing a small, strong shot topped with a golden layer of crema. It is the base of almost every other drink on this page.

02

Cappuccino

A traditional cappuccino is built from three equal parts: espresso, steamed milk and milk foam. The thick foam on top gives it a light texture, while the espresso keeps the coffee flavour bold.

03

Latte

A latte is espresso with a generous amount of steamed milk and only a thin layer of foam. With more milk than a cappuccino, it tastes smoother and milder, which makes it an easy cup to sip slowly.

04

Cold Coffee

Cold coffee is brewed coffee served chilled over ice, usually mixed with milk. It is refreshing, lightly sweet when sugar is added, and a popular choice when the weather is too warm for a hot cup.

05

Americano

An americano is a shot of espresso topped up with hot water. The water softens the intensity and gives a longer drink with no milk, so the flavour of the coffee itself stays front and centre.

06

Mocha

A mocha combines espresso, steamed milk and chocolate. The chocolate rounds off the bitterness of the coffee, giving a rich, dessert-like cup for anyone who likes their coffee on the sweeter side.
